What is GrapheneTrace?

GrapheneTrace transforms raw foot pressure data into deep clinical insights. Built for patients, clinicians, and administrators, it offers a full ecosystem of analysis tools, real-time metrics, and intelligent alerts.

Insights & Features

Real-Time Metrics

Instantly track clinical markers—peak pressure, contact area, gait symmetry, and more.

Heatmap Visualisation

View 32×32 pressure grids with playback for injury monitoring and rehabilitation tracking.

Smart Alerts

Automatic warnings for dangerous pressure, low contact, and risk events.
